Key Design Rules (English units)

  • Gas yield ≈ 35–40 scf per lb dry wood
  • Air ≈ 1.0–1.5 lb per lb dry wood (ER 0.25–0.35)
  • Nozzle air velocity 100–115 ft/s
  • Reduction zone height minimum 8 in
  • Insulation is not optional for reliable low-tar operation
  • Engine-quality tar target typically under 100 mg/Nm³
  • Model uses simplified Boudouard + water-gas shift equilibrium — indicative only
