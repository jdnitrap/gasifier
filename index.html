5. Reactor Sizing

Key Design Rules

  • Gas yield ≈ 35–40 scf per lb dry wood
  • Air ≈ 1.0–1.5 lb per lb dry wood (ER 0.25–0.35)
  • Nozzle velocity 100–115 ft/s · Reduction zone ≥ 8 in
  • Insulation is critical for low tar
  • Engine-quality tar target typically < 100 mg/Nm³
  • Model is simplified equilibrium — use for insight, not final design
