# Wood Gasifier Design Reference Data (English Units)

## Hearth Load Guidelines (Imbert / Downdraft)

| Parameter                  | Value                          | Notes |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------|-------|
| B_g max (continuous)      | ~2.95 scf/h per in²           | Higher → high pressure drop |
| B_g min (low tar)         | ~1.0 – 1.15 scf/h per in²     | Below this → excessive tar |
| Typical design point      | 2.3 – 2.6 scf/h per in²       | Good balance |
| Turndown ratio            | 2.5 – 3                       | Standard Imbert |

Gas yield approximation: **35–40 standard cubic feet (scf)** of producer gas per pound of dry wood.

## Geometry Rules of Thumb

- Nozzle plane height above throat: 1.0–1.6 × throat diameter (min ~4 inches)
- Reduction zone height: at least 8 inches (historical average ~12–13 inches)
- Hearth diameter at nozzle level: throat diameter + 4 inches (single) or + 8 inches (double/Imbert)
- Throat cone angle: 45–60°
- Nozzle air velocity: 70–115 ft/s (prefer 100–115 ft/s)

## Producer Gas Typical Values (Downdraft, Wood)

| Component     | Range (vol%) | Typical |
|---------------|--------------|---------|
| CO            | 17–25        | 20      |
| H₂            | 12–20        | 16      |
| CH₄           | 1–3          | 2       |
| CO₂           | 9–15         | 12      |
| N₂            | 45–55        | 50      |
| LHV           | 120–160 BTU/scf | 135–150 BTU/scf |

## Fuel Notes

- Ideal moisture: under 15–20% (wet basis)
- Bulk density of prepared wood: roughly 9–19 lb/ft³
- Preferred particle size for small units: ¾ inch to 2 inches

## Quick Conversion Helpers

- 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb
- 1 Nm³ ≈ 35.3 scf
- 1 MJ ≈ 948 BTU
- 1 kW ≈ 3,412 BTU/h
- 1 m/s ≈ 3.28 ft/s
- 1 cm² ≈ 0.155 in²
